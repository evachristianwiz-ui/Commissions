Cheroisé Story

Cheroise Furniture Factory emerged from the shared vision of Eng. Bishoy Ebrahim and Eng. Peter Edward, blending engineering precision with design finesse. Their entrepreneurial journey began with a commitment to redefine the furniture industry, not merely as manufacturers but as artisans of functional elegance.

At the core of Cheroisé Furniture Factory is a culture of collaboration. Eng. Bishoy and Eng. Peter fostered an environment where skilled artisans and designers work in synergy, pooling their expertise to create pieces that transcend mere functionality.
